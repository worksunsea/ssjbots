01

What a "demand" is

A contact is a person — their phone number, name, city, birthday, tags. A demand is one specific purchase enquiry belonging to that contact — a particular thing they want to buy, for a particular occasion, at a particular budget.

One contact can have several demands over time: the same client might enquire about a wedding necklace in March and a gift ring in December — two separate demands, one contact record.

Why this matters: when you open a demand, you're looking at one enquiry's story — not the client's whole history. Their contact record (tags, family, past visits) sits inside the same screen so you always have the fuller picture too.
02

Where demands come from

🤖 WhatsApp bot (most common)

A customer messages the shop's WhatsApp number. The bot chats with them, works out what they're looking for, and automatically creates the demand — no telecaller action needed at this stage. The bot keeps messaging until it either qualifies them (sets a call step) or hands off to a human.

🏪 Walk-in

A customer walks into the shop. Staff use + Walk-in in the Demands tab to log them — covered fully in Chapter 11.

A manager can also add a demand manually via + New Demand (for phone enquiries, referrals, etc.) — as a telecaller you'll rarely need this button yourself.

03

Temperature — hot, warm, cold

Every demand gets an automatic temperature. It's the system doing triage for you — always work hot before warm before cold.

🔥 HotNeeds a human right now — call is due, visit is within 36 hours, bot handed off, or the lead is brand new (under an hour old)
🌤 WarmActive conversation in the last 24 hours, visit within a week, or a returning client (who never auto-drops to cold)
❄️ ColdOpen, but quiet for over a day — still worth a call, just not urgent
✅ ConvertedSale closed
💀 DeadLost, junk, or opted out

A manager can manually pin a temperature (🔥 / 🌤 / ❄️) inside the full record when they know something the system doesn't — a pinned temperature overrides the automatic one until it's removed.

04

Reading a demand card

Every card in your list boils down to two lines and one button.

Priya Sharma 🔥 Hot
📞 Call now · overdue 2h
Wedding necklace in polki · Wedding 15 Dec · ₹5,00,000

Line one: name, temperature chip, a gold ⭐ VIP chip if they're a returning client, and the button. Line two: what they want · occasion + date · budget, in plain text.

The button is always the single correct thing to do next. It's computed from the demand's state, so you never have to work out priority yourself:

05

The funnel — bot steps vs. call steps

Behind every demand is a funnel — a sequence of steps that decides what happens next. Two kinds of step exist:

💬 Message step→ 📞 Call step→ 💬 Message step→ 🏁 Done
  • 💬 Message steps — the bot sends WhatsApp messages on a timer. No action needed from you; the card shows "🤖 Bot chatting · no action."
  • 📞 Call steps — this is where you come in. The system schedules a call time and the card's button turns into a call prompt when it's due.

Managers can see and change the full funnel picture (current step, next step, step history) behind a "🛤 Funnel ▾" disclosure in the full record — as a telecaller you don't need to touch this; just work the button in front of you.

06

Logging a call, start to finish

  1. Tap the red/orange call button on the card (or inside the record). The call-log form opens straight away — you don't need to find it in a menu.
  2. A script appears on the right, already filled in with the client's name and product. Which script you get depends on the attempt number: S1 for the first call, S2 for the middle attempts, S3 for the last one or two. Below it, an objections cheat-sheet of common pushbacks and suggested responses.
  3. A timer starts the moment the form opens, so call duration is tracked automatically — you don't fill this in.
  4. Make the call, then record what happened as the disposition:
    • ✅ Answered — interested (moves the demand forward to messaging)
    • 🕒 Answered — not now (callback)
    • ❌ Answered — not interested
    • 🔕 No answer
    • 📞 Busy (system retries in 15 min)
    • 📩 Voicemail left
    • 📅 Callback requested
    • 🚫 Wrong number
    • ⛔ Do not call (DNC)
  5. If you picked "Answered — not now" or "Callback requested", you must set a callback time — this is exactly what makes the card turn red again at the right moment later.
  6. Add notes — anything specific they said. Future-you (or whoever picks this up next) will thank you.
  7. Save. The card and the demand's cadence counter both update immediately.
Tip: read the script before dialling, not while the phone is ringing.
07

Call cadence & what happens if no one answers

Every call-step demand has a maximum number of attempts (set by the shop, commonly 6). The full record shows a dot tracker — filled dots for attempts used, empty for attempts remaining — plus when the next attempt is due.

If the customer never answers and the cadence is exhausted, the system automatically moves the demand into a cold_revive path instead of just abandoning it — it isn't lost, it drops in priority and switches to a longer-term nurture flow.

If you called and there was no answer, you can send a one-tap "📲 Send 'tried to call' WA" message from the call-cadence section — a polite WhatsApp letting them know you tried, so the silence on their end doesn't feel like being ignored.

08

Scheduling and running a visit

Open the full record → ⋯ More → 📅 Schedule visit. Pick a date and time and, optionally, a note for the salesperson who'll host the visit (e.g. "coming with husband, wants to see polki set"). The demand will now surface in your queue on that day.

From there, the card's button carries you through automatically:

  • 🟠 Confirm visit for tomorrow — call or WhatsApp to confirm they're still coming.
  • 🟢 Visit TODAY — prepare pieces — the day has arrived; review the demand notes before they walk in.
  • 🟠 Visited — mark result — appears automatically once the visit time has passed with no outcome logged yet. Open the record and close the demand (Chapter 10) with what actually happened.
09

Sending a design reference

Open the full record → ⋯ More → 📤 Send design. Paste an image/file link, add a caption if you like, and send — it goes out over the shop's connected WhatsApp session and is logged against the demand automatically, so anyone opening the record later can see what was shared and when.

10

Closing a demand

Open the full record → Close demand ▾:

✅Converted

They bought. Triggers the post-sale sequence automatically — a thank-you, then a feedback check-in, then a review request, then a longer-term check-in.

❌Lost…

Didn't buy — pick the real reason, it matters for what management sees later:

💰 Price too high
⏰ Bad timing / not ready
🏪 Went to competitor
🚫 Not interested at all
💸 Budget too low
👻 No show / ghosted
🗑 Junk / wrong number
🤔Not interested

They engaged but genuinely don't want to proceed — softer than Lost, no reason needed.

🗑Junk

Not a real enquiry at all.

🏷Supplier

Wrong category — this was a vendor/supplier message, not a customer.

🚫Opt Out / DNC

They asked to never be contacted again. Blocks all future calls and messages.

Cannot be undone: Opt Out / DNC is permanent. Only use it when the customer explicitly asked to be left alone.
11

Adding a walk-in customer

Click + Walk-in at the top of the Demands tab. This is built to fill in about thirty seconds, while the customer is still standing at the counter.

aNamerequired
bPhonerequired
cProduct categorygold, diamond, silver…
dEstimate / budgetrough figure is fine
eDescriptionwhat they're looking for
fOccasion + dateif there is one

Press Save Walk-in. Contact and demand are both created, and the card appears in your list right away.

Everything else is optional — city, birthday, exchange/trade-in, items seen, price quoted, competitor mentioned, design reference photo, and more. It's collapsed under "▼ Complete visit details" at the bottom of the same form. Open it if you have a spare minute right then; otherwise leave it closed — saving never requires it.

If the phone number already exists in the system, you'll be offered the existing contact instead of creating a duplicate — always use the existing one unless you're certain it's a different person.

12

Returning clients & VIPs

When a contact is marked as a known client (has bought before), their demand card gets a gold ⭐ VIP chip, and the full record shows a yellow banner:

⭐ Returning client — known customer, treat as priority. Confirm their previous purchase preference before calling.

Returning clients also never automatically cool down to ❄️ Cold, even if they've gone quiet — the system keeps them warm until a human decides otherwise. Past estimates and visit history are visible in the record so you can reference what they've bought or asked about before.

13

Tags, family members, hidden numbers

Tags

Every contact can carry tags (source tags like walk_in, plus custom ones the shop has set up). Tags live on the contact, not the demand — visible and editable at the bottom of the full record.

Family members

The record also shows linked family members for the contact — useful for gifting occasions and for spotting when two enquiries are actually the same household.

Hidden numbers (LID)

Occasionally WhatsApp hides a sender's real phone number and shows a "WA hidden #" placeholder instead. You'll see an orange banner: "⚠️ This is a WA-hidden sender. Real phone unknown." If you learn their real number in conversation, use 🔗 Link to existing contact to merge it into their proper contact record — otherwise you'll never be able to call them directly.

14

Common situations & what to do

The button says "Call at 5:30pm" and it's greyed out — can I still call early?
Yes, calling early is fine — the button is just telling you it's not urgent yet. Open the record and use Log Call from there.
I found a duplicate — the same person has two contact records.
Ask a manager to merge them (⋯ More → Merge duplicate, manager-only) — merging moves all messages and demands onto one record so history isn't lost.
The customer wants to speak to a manager, not the bot or me.
A manager can set the lead to Handoff, which pauses the bot and flags it — you'll then see "💬 Reply personally — bot handed off" until it's resolved.
I marked the wrong disposition on a call.
Log a fresh call attempt with the correct outcome and note the correction — call logs are kept as history, not edited after the fact.
A demand looks stuck — no button, no bot activity, nothing.
Open the record — if the funnel section shows "⚠ No step set yet," the bot will assign the first step automatically the next time it replies. If it's been quiet a long time, flag it to a manager.
